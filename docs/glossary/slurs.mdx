# Derogatory Terms

A list of somewhat commonly encountered derogatory words and phrases (a.k.a "slurs") that one might come across when navigating through conversations that involve the queer community.

You may sometimes come across posts where someone calls themselves with some of these terms, they might simply be trying to vent their frustration or something, I can't tell for sure.

However, <abbr title='in my honest opinion'>imho</abbr>, it's generally better to avoid using these terms, except for the purpose of education.

I say "education" because it is important to equip ourselves with sufficient knowledge to:
1. Avoid accidentally using it on another person
2. Know how to protect ourselves and be aware when we are being called with derogatory terms.
