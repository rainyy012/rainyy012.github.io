It's been a while since I embraced the fact that I'm trans. Since then, life has changed so much, but every now and then I still find myself feeling sentimental about my life prior to that.

Of course, pretending that nothing had happened is technically an option, but I'm not that delusional and I can't stay ignorant forever. I knew I had to move forward and make a lot of involuntary changes. It's just that… the peacefulness and simplicity of life back then are really the things that I missed.

Welp, enough yapping. ( - ω - )

I'm writing this post to share some of the songs that I've been listening to lately to… well, just cope. Sometimes, they remind me to be grateful for how far I've come and that it's okay to feel tired or hurt; other times, they help me release my emotions, and sometimes they simply put my mind at ease.

## 1. HACHI - Rainy proof (Acoustic ver.)

<YTEmbed videoId='x6OQqeOBZGc' />

This is a love song by vTuber HACHI, and it's not about being trans at all (in fact, _none_ of the songs mentioned in this post are). I especially love the acoustic version, maybe because I play guitar too, but more importantly, I think the silence in this version gives me space to process my emotions whenever I listen to it. (You can find the [original version](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LcwIMiaW-Tk) and [English translation](https://www.lyrical-nonsense.com/global/lyrics/hachi-vsinger/rainy-proof/english) here.)

The intro makes me feel like "returning to calmness after a conflict", as if many bad things happened one after another, and even though none of the problems have been resolved, at least the chaos has settled down…

Also, I wonder if this song is what I subconsciously got inspiration for my name from?

## 2. Lucas Music - Fly a letter to the wind

<YTEmbed videoId='DpBPDEY49eo' />

I first heard this song from that fan-made Genshin Impact video titled [Our Journey Ended 10 Years Ago](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ubECKgPunCs).

The "original" source seems to trace back to a channel named "Lucas Music", whose video is embedded above, but even so, it still sounds like a crude edit based on another (more genuine) source that I am not aware of.

But when you're feeling down, who really cares? That sunset scene really sets the mood, and the crude edit adds a bit of human touch. The song loops every 21 seconds with a slightly imperfect cut, almost like it's reminding us that we're all imperfect creatures.

(Link to [original version by JayM](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4JXUlGSHMfw))

[Bonus video of Furina playing this song on the Windsong Lyre](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JeEf_8moZG4)

## 3. Kyo Hanabasami - Fragile

<YTEmbed videoId='O_-YCy51H6Y' />

In this song, it's the lyrics that really struck me. It's about putting on a brave face and pretending everything's okay, even though the heart is so fragile that a single word could still hurt it very badly. 

Putting in effort to transition, putting in effort to pass, seeking recognition from people close to us such as family members and friends in hope that they would accept us for who we are… acting like everything is easy and fine, only to be called names, denied our feelings, or simply misunderstood… doesn't that sound familiar? 🫣

I often feel lost in a sea of doubts, sometimes even questioning myself "why am I even trying so hard?", and I haven't found a satisfying answer yet. For now, I push forward out of spite, for what life as a trans person is still worth, for the friendships I might forge along the way, and for the regrets I do not wish to have…

The song also reminds me of when I first heard it. It was a time when I still felt like it was worth putting in effort for something, hoping that the results would be worth all the pain.

## 4. (Bonus) stargazing 💫 shiloh type beats

<YTEmbed videoId='fuaAG1pW27o' />

When I first came to realize that I am trans (egg-hatching moment), I began to go into a downward spiral. All of a sudden, there were so many things to process at once.

_"How am I going to eventually come out at work?"_

_"My current work doesn't seem to be paying enough long term…"_

_"I need to get on HRT as soon as possible…"_

_"I want to be able to afford surgery too…"_

_"I needed to move out in case my parents do not receive it well…"_

These songs had a strange calming effect that temporarily numbed my pain. The lyrics didn't even matter at that point. When you're sad, the brain just takes what clicks with it and filters out the rest.

Some of these songs "aided" the downward spiral (the mental descend was inevitable but at least they made it not as rough), some, after listening made me feel like I gained a bit of clarity back again.

One song in particular hit hard — ["wish I could tell my mum"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fuaAG1pW27o&t=1391). My mum is still alive and well at the time of writing, she is a very kind person. I just wish I could tell her properly and not leave [regrets](#:~:text=for%20the%20regrets%20I%20do%20not%20wish%20to%20have…), after all, life is _Fragile_ and we won't know when it might be the last time we get to talk to one another.
