# About

Hello, I'm Rain and I go by the she/her pronouns.

A lot has been going on in my mind since <a href='/resources/glossary/main#:~:text=Egg,denial%20about%20it'>since I hatched</a> and I needed a way to express them. As I finally started to be honest with myself, I begin to explore and learn about the queer community in depth.

So, I've decided to start this blog as a place to share my thoughts, memes, or just general knowledge that I've learned along the way that I think will be relevant to the community for a long time. Enjoy!
